SLIDE 02 | SYSTEM BACKGROUND

Introduction — Industrial Motors & Maintenance

Context & Need
⚠️ The Critical Role & Failure Risks
  • ● Industrial motors drive pumps, conveyors, compressors, fans, and assembly lines across manufacturing.
  • ● Continuous 24/7 high-load operation leads to insulation thermal breakdown and mechanical bearing wear.
  • ✖ Severe Downtime: An unexpected failure halts production lines instantly.
  • ✖ Escalating Costs: Emergency secondary equipment repairs are 10x more costly.
  • ✖ Safety Hazards: Thermal runaway or sudden rotor lock causes workplace hazards.
💡 Our Proposed IoT Solution
  • ✔ Continuous Telemetry: ESP32 gathers thermal, vibration, current, and optical readings every 2 seconds.
  • ✔ Wireless Streaming: Telemetry transmitted over local Wi-Fi to a high-speed FastAPI backend.
  • ✔ Single-Pane Dashboard: Real-time visual meters and interactive historical charts for plant engineers.
  • ✔ Predictive AI: ML models detect abnormal degradation patterns before catastrophic motor lock.

“Our system continuously monitors motor condition using sensors. The ESP32 collects telemetry over Wi-Fi, the dashboard provides live operator insights, and ML predicts impending faults.”

SLIDE 05 | PROBLEM DEFINITION

Problem Statement — Traditional vs Modern Maintenance

Comparative Analysis
❌ Traditional Maintenance Deficiencies
  • ✖ Periodic Manual Inspection: Faults developing between scheduled visits remain hidden until breakdown.
  • ✖ Undetected Thermal Drift: Gradual winding insulation degradation goes unnoticed until burnout.
  • ✖ Vibration Blindspots: Bearing raceway flaking and shaft misalignment are impossible to measure by ear.
  • ✖ High Labor Overhead: Technicians spend hours walking plant floors taking manual spot checks.
✔️ Proposed IoT Condition-Based Platform
  • ✔ Continuous 24/7 Telemetry: Sub-second sampling of current, vibration, and temperature.
  • ✔ Edge Threshold Alarms: Immediate automated trip alerts on abnormal current spikes or vibration jumps.
  • ✔ Calibrated Accuracy: 1.8925 V zero-current offset calibration ensures accurate load current readings.
  • ✔ Predictive AI Forecasting: Machine learning predicts Remaining Useful Life (RUL) before failure.

System Objectives

Controller & Temperature

  • ● Integrated 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi: Transmits sensor packets directly to the cloud without needing external shields.
  • ● 12-bit Multi-Channel ADC: Resolves analog voltage signals with 4096 quantization levels.

  • ● Thermal Range: -40°C to +80°C with ±0.5°C high accuracy.
  • ● Humidity Range: 0 to 100% RH with ±2% precision.
  • ● Insulation Protection: Motor winding insulation degradation follows the Arrhenius law—a 10°C thermal increase halves insulation lifespan!
  • ● Early Warning: Detects ventilation blockages and excessive ambient humidity.
SLIDE 10 | ELECTRICAL SENSING

ACS712 Current Sensor & Calibration

Load Current Analysis
⚡ Hall-Effect Sensing Principle
  • ● Galvanic Isolation: Measures motor current without electrical connection between high-power motor loop and ESP32.
  • ● Load Current Profiles:
    • Inrush Current: High current surge during motor startup.
    • Nominal Load: Stable baseline current under regular torque.
    • Overload / Stall: Sustained elevated current indicating mechanical jam.
📐 Mathematical Model & Calibration
// Hardware Calibrated Zero-Current Voltage const float ZERO_V = 1.8925; // Calibrated Zero Point const float SENSITIVITY = 0.100; // 100 mV/A (20A Module) // Conversion in ESP32 Loop: float adcVoltage = (analogRead(34) / 4095.0) * 3.3 * DIVIDER; float motorCurrent = abs((adcVoltage - ZERO_V) / SENSITIVITY);

* 100-sample running average filter eliminates motor brush noise and ADC quantization jitter.

SLIDE 11 | MECHANICAL SENSING

MPU6050 Vibration & Acceleration Analysis

Vibration Vector Math
📐 Acceleration Vector Mathematics

Total dynamic acceleration vector is computed from 3-axis MEMS accelerometer readings:

Total Acceleration = √( X² + Y² + Z² ) Vibration Magnitude = | Total Acceleration - 1.0g |

Stationary motor experiences 1.0g gravity. Imbalance or bearing defects create high-frequency dynamic oscillations deviating from 1.0g.

📊 Vibration Severity Classification
  • ●
    LOW (< 0.05 g): Normal smooth running, balanced rotor, healthy bearings.
  • ●
    MEDIUM (0.05 – 0.15 g): Developing minor imbalance, looseness, or slight misalignment. Warning logged.
  • ●
    HIGH (≥ 0.15 g): Severe vibration! Imminent bearing raceway damage or structural fault. Alarm triggered.
SLIDE 12 | OPTICAL & ACTUATOR

IR Optical Sensor & L298N Motor Driver

Actuation & Position
👁️ IR Optical Sensor (GPIO 35)
  • ● Infrared Reflex Pair: Detects physical object presence on conveyor line or gear teeth.
  • ● RPM & Speed Gating: Paired with slotted encoder disk to measure rotational velocity.
  • ● Fault Correlation: Distinguishes between electrical motor spinning vs mechanically locked rotor.
⚙️ L298N Dual H-Bridge Driver
  • ● High-Power Stage: Handles up to 46V and 2A continuous motor loads.
  • ● ESP32 Pin Interface:
    • ENA (GPIO 25): PWM speed modulation (0–255 duty cycle).
    • IN1 (GPIO 26): Forward direction phase.
    • IN2 (GPIO 27): Reverse direction phase.
  • ● Emergency Safety Trip: Allows remote or automated shutdown upon critical vibration alarm.

SLIDE 15 | EMBEDDED PROGRAMMING

ESP32 Firmware Execution Lifecycle

Firmware Logic
🔄 Non-Blocking Execution Loop
  • 1. Setup(): Initialize I2C (MPU6050), DHT22, GPIO pins, and connect to Wi-Fi SSID.
  • 2. Sample ADC: Read ACS712 over 100 samples for RMS current computation.
  • 3. Acquire IMU: Read X, Y, Z acceleration and compute vibration severity.
  • 4. Read DHT22: Acquire temperature (°C) and humidity (%).
  • 5. Transmit JSON: Post telemetry packet to /api/telemetry.
📦 Sample Telemetry JSON Payload

SLIDE 16 | SIGNAL PROCESSING

Edge Sensor Data Processing & Filtering

Algorithms & Math
⚡ Current Conversion

1. Sample 12-bit ADC (0–4095).
2. Compute pin voltage.
3. Subtract calibrated zero-point offset (1.8925 V).
4. Divide by 100 mV/A sensitivity.
5. Moving average filtering rejects motor commutation noise.

📈 Vibration Extraction

1. Read raw 16-bit registers for X, Y, Z.
2. Convert LSB to g-acceleration.
3. Vector norm: √(X² + Y² + Z²)
4. Compute deviation from 1.0g baseline.
5. Classify severity level.

🚨 Threshold Rules Engine

Temperature:
• <50°C Normal | 50-70°C Warn | >70°C Alarm

Vibration:
• <0.05g Low | 0.05-0.15g Med | ≥0.15g High

Current:
• >4.5A Overload Trip

SLIDE 19 | PREDICTIVE INTELLIGENCE

Predictive Maintenance & Machine Learning

AI Fault Prediction
🧠 Predictive ML Pipeline
  • 1. Data Ingestion: Continuous time-series aggregation across all sensors.
  • 2. Feature Engineering: RMS, peak-to-peak amplitude, crest factor, and FFT spectral peaks.
  • 3. Classification Models: Random Forest / Gradient Boosting for multi-class fault isolation.
  • 4. Failure Probability: Outputs health index (%) and Remaining Useful Life (RUL).
📈 Health Condition States
  • ●
    NORMAL (Health 95-100%): All physical parameters within nominal operating bands.
  • ●
    WARNING (Health 70-94%): Subtle vibration elevation (0.05-0.15g) or gradual thermal drift. Maintenance scheduled.
  • ●
    CRITICAL ANOMALY (Health <70%): High vibration (>0.15g) or overcurrent. Automated trip engaged.
